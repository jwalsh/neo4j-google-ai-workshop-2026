* Framework

** Copy the directory

A template repository or a generator does not beat =cp -r= here, for three
reasons. There are two data points at most: this repository was itself
"scaffolded during the session from the shape of =babashka-workshop-spectre="
(=.meta/README.md=), and that worked in an afternoon. The reusable part is
small, roughly 600 of 2,400 lines in =src/workshop/=. And the owner's own
rule is that a synthesis waits for two repositories to agree; a template
written from one freezes its accidents. What copying needs is for the
generic and the specific to be in different files, so that the copy is
followed by a delete and not by surgery. That is the whole proposal.
