* Neo4j and AI Reference Architecture

Redrawn as [[file:diagrams/neo4j-and-ai-reference-architecture.mmd][diagrams/neo4j-and-ai-reference-architecture.mmd]].

Five columns, left to right:

- Data Sources: structured (databases, JSON, XML, CSV), ontologies (RDF),
  unstructured (documents, conversations, video).
- Creation: Gemini and a Neo4j Agent.
- Knowledge Graph: Neo4j Aura, holding Query, the graph database and
  Document Intelligence.
- Consumption: Gemini and a Neo4j Agent again.
- Applications: enterprise knowledge search, customer service, ticket
  triaging, recommendations, news content and discovery, FAQ bots, patient
  prioritization, clinical decision support systems, pharmacovigilance,
  health assistants.

The arrows run inward from both ends: blue from the data sources through
creation into the graph, green from the applications through consumption
toward it.

Where today sits on it: Lab 4 was the structured row, a CSV, loaded without
the Creation column at all. Lab 6 is the unstructured row through Document
Intelligence. The ontologies row, RDF, has no lab; the experiments under
=experiments/= that hold the Form 13 data as triples are this repository's
look at it.

* Enterprise Agentic Reference Architecture

Redrawn as [[file:diagrams/enterprise-agentic-reference-architecture.mmd][diagrams/enterprise-agentic-reference-architecture.mmd]].

- Across the top: iApps, Agents, Tools.
- Down the left: Agentic Orchestration, as a column of vendor logos: Amazon
  Bedrock AgentCore, Gemini Enterprise, Microsoft Foundry, Agentforce,
  Agent Bricks, LangGraph, Snowflake Cortex Agents.
- Under the top band: a Knowledge Layer "powered by Neo4j Graph
  Intelligence Platform", made of Ontologies, Data and Memory.
- Under that, five blocks each joined to the knowledge layer by a
  double-headed arrow: Databases (drawn as kinds: relational, key-value,
  document, graph, wide-column, in-memory, time-series, object-oriented,
  blob, ledger, vector), Lakehouse Platform (Databricks, Snowflake,
  BigQuery, Microsoft Fabric), Apps & Services, AI/ML Ops (Vertex AI,
  Databricks, Amazon SageMaker, Azure Machine Learning), Catalog (AWS Glue,
  Unity Catalog, Microsoft Purview, Dataplex, Collibra, and one logo not
  read).
- Along the bottom: Real-time Messaging & Processing (clickstream, IoT,
  CDC): Spark, Kafka, Amazon Kinesis, Cloud Pub/Sub, with arrows up into
  Databases and the Lakehouse.

The claim of the picture is where the graph goes: between the agents and
every other store, as the one layer they all read through. Gemini
Enterprise, which today's labs use, is one of seven orchestrators in the
left column. That is a reading of the layout; the slide has no caption.
