Return every entity once, named as the text names it, without trademark symbols.

Entity types:
- Organization: the company the filing is about.
- Segment: a product category or line the filing groups products under (for example a heading such as "Wearables, Home and Accessories", or a category named inside it such as "Home").
- Product: a named device or piece of software.
- Service: a named service.
- Competitor: a company or kind of company the filing says it competes with.

Relationship types, with their direction:
- Organization OFFERS Product or Service.
- Organization HAS_SEGMENT Segment.
- Segment INCLUDES Product, Service or another Segment. When the text says a category "includes" named items, write one INCLUDES relationship per item.
- Product RUNS_ON Product, for a device and its operating system.
- Organization COMPETES_WITH Competitor.

source and target must be names that appear in entities. evidence is the short phrase from the text that supports the relationship, copied exactly.
