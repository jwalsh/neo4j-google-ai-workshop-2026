   `__Entity__.embedding`, `community_vector` on `__Community__.embedding`,
   384 dimensions, cosine), with no nodes under them. By 19:49 there were 1
   Document, 9 Chunk and 71 `__Entity__` nodes (Product 36, Segment 16,
