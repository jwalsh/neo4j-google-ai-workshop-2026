#+TITLE: 013 - The agents on the laptop's own models, asked once and asked twice
#+DATE: 2026-10-09

* Source

Experiment 009 measured the tool-choosing agent with models on another
machine. On 2026-10-09 that machine could not be reached from a JVM here,
and the laptop had two small models of its own: =qwen2.5:3b= and
=qwen3:4b=. The first question put to the transit agent through either,
"How do I get from Maverick to Kendall?", chose the right tool and gave it
no stations. =workshop.agent/choose= gained a second question for that
case, and this is the measurement of it.

* Hypothesis

The model's first answer is held to one schema for every tool: a tool name
and an arguments object with every parameter of every tool, none required.
A small model picks the tool well and fills the arguments badly. Asking a
second time, for the chosen tool's arguments alone, with the ones it needs
required and with the stored values as an enum where the graph has 400 or
fewer of them (=agent/argument-schema=, =agent/domains=), turns refusals
into answers and adds no wrong ones.

* Gate

42 questions: the 12 for filings and the 12 for papers from experiment
009, unchanged, and 18 new ones for transit in =questions-mbta.edn=, five
of which say a station loosely or out of order. Graded as 009 grades:
right, refused, wrong. Each model runs them twice, =:single-call true=
and not.

The gate is more right answers with two calls than with one, for both
models, and no more wrong ones.

* Result

Half passed. More right for both models; one more wrong for one of them.
One pass, temperature 0, M1 Pro with 16 GB, four Neo4j JVMs running
beside the model.

|------------+-------+-------+---------+-------+----+-------------+----------------|
| qwen2.5:3b | one   |    33 |       8 |     1 | 42 |          34 |           0.63 |
| qwen2.5:3b | two   |    38 |       3 |     1 | 42 |          34 |           1.24 |
| qwen3:4b   | one   |    36 |       5 |     1 | 42 |          37 |           1.04 |
| qwen3:4b   | two   |    40 |       0 |     2 | 42 |          37 |           1.72 |

"Tool chosen" is the model's first answer alone: the expected tool, or

model, since the second call never changes the tool.

* What the failures say

- All of the gain is the five journey questions. Asked once, neither
  model gave a journey its two stations, ten refusals of ten. Asked twice,
  nine of ten are right. It is the only tool here with two required
  arguments of the same kind, and the flat schema is where they are lost.
- The new wrong answer: "To South Station from Wonderland, please." came
  back from =qwen3:4b= as a journey from South Station to Wonderland. Both
  names are in the question, so grounding passes it, and the rows look
  like an answer. =qwen2.5:3b= got the direction right. A check for it
  would have to read the question's grammar, which is the model's job.
- "When is the next train from Park Street?" is wrong from both models in
  both modes: it runs "Which Routes Serve a Station". No tool knows a
  timetable and the model does not say "none". This is 009's finding
  again, a wrong choice passes through a tool whose one argument the
  question happens to supply.
- =qwen2.5:3b= still loses three filings questions, the same three either
  way. Two are the pair of tool names that differ by an apostrophe (009).
  The third is "Who owns 2U Inc?", written back as "2U, Inc." with a
  comma the question does not have, and dropped. Company names are too
  many for an enum, so the second call cannot help there.
- Where the second call offered a list and the question named nothing on
  it, the model picked a real manager anyway (GOLDENTREE ASSET MANAGEMENT
  LP). An enum removes "invented" and leaves "not asked for". The
  shared-word rule in =grounded= dropped it.
- The second call about doubles the time and is made whenever a list
  exists, needed or not.

* Log

1. Both models, transit example, one call: right tool, no stations.
2. =argument-schema= and the second call when a needed argument is
   missing. Both models answer the example.
3. =domains=: stored values as an enum in the second call; =grounded=
   lets a listed value stand on a shared word. "Gov Center", "Park St",
   "the airport" resolve.
4. The run above.

* Not done

- Books. The question set would name titles from a personal library, and
  those stay out of the repository.
- A 7B model on this machine. 009's best, qwen2.5-coder 7B, scored 11 and
  12 of 12 on filings and papers with one call; it has not been run on
  transit or on the laptop.
- Variance: one run per row.
- The second call only when the first one's arguments fail, which would
  save the time on the questions that were already right.
- A tool that says no: a timetable question has nowhere right to go.
