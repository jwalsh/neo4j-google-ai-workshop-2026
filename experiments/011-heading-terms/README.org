#+TITLE: 011 - Terms from book headings: spaCy beside a rule, and what was ported
#+DATE: 2026-10-09

* Source

The 7,036 bookmark headings of the 27 books on =shelves/homage.edn= that
have an outline (two of the 29 have none), read by =bb books outline= into
the cache. The rule is =workshop.books/words=. The Python is
=terms_spacy.py= here, spaCy 3.8.16 with =en_core_web_sm= 3.8.0.

* Hypothesis

The books graph joins books through terms, and a term was whatever a
regular expression and a stop list let through. A tagger that knows a noun
from a verb, and a phrase from two words, makes better terms: fewer that
are not subjects, and multiword ones the rule cannot see.

* Gate

Python is for finding out; what survives is Clojure (=CLAUDE.md=). So the
gate is on the port: after it, the rule finds at least half of spaCy's
shared phrases, and the ten most similar pairs of books agree with spaCy's
on at least eight.

* Result

Passed on the pairs, by a little on the phrases, with the tagger's main
advantage left unported.

| measure                                   | rule before | spaCy | rule after |
|-------------------------------------------+-------------+-------+------------|
| single terms used by two or more books    |       1,285 |   938 |      1,284 |
| of those, in both lists                   |         773 |     - |        784 |
| phrases used by two or more books         |           0 |   105 |        191 |
| of those, in both lists                   |           - |     - |         61 |
| ten most similar pairs, shared with spaCy |      8 of 10 |     - | not rerun  |

The two lists of pairs differ where spaCy does better: it puts Cloud
Observability in Action beside Software Telemetry, and the rule did not.

* What spaCy showed

- 512 of the rule's terms were not spaCy's. Most are not subjects: "adding",
  "across", "automatically", "applying". The rule has no way to know a verb.
- Some were the rule's own damage. It dropped every final s: "anonymou",
  "approache", "aritie", "asynchronou".
- 165 of spaCy's were not the rule's, and most of those are spaCy's
  mistakes on this text. A heading in title case reads as proper nouns, so
  "Agents" keeps its s; and it has no idea that "colophon", "appendix" and
  "cover illustration" are a book's furniture.
- The phrases are the find: "retrieval augmented generation" in seven
  books, "large language model" and "prompt engineering" in five, "graph
  database", "prompt injection", "fine tuning".

* What was ported, and what was not

- Ported: plural folding by ending (=books/singular=), with the exceptions
  the bad cases named.
- Ported: =books/phrases=, runs of two or three subject words that stand
  next to each other. No tagger; the filter is the same as for a word, two
  books must use it. It finds 191, of which spaCy has 61. Its extras are
  mostly real ("worker node", "retention policy", "text embedding") and
  some are not ("source versus", "catch finally").
- Not ported: part of speech. The 500 verb-like terms are still terms.
  A stop list cannot fix that without losing "logging", "testing" and
  "embedding", which end the same way and are subjects.
- Not ported: lemmas. "embedding model" and spaCy's "embed model" are the
  same phrase and count as different.

* Log

1. =headings.json= written from Clojure, so both sides read the same
   headings and the rule's own words.
2. spaCy over 7,036 headings in a few seconds, numbering stripped first.
3. The comparison above. Nothing was tuned on the Python side.
4. The port, and tests for the endings that were wrong.
5. The books graph reloaded: 1,475 terms, 12,579 ABOUT edges.

* Run

#+begin_src sh
uv run --with spacy \
  --with 'en_core_web_sm @ https://github.com/explosion/spacy-models/releases/download/en_core_web_sm-3.8.0/en_core_web_sm-3.8.0-py3-none-any.whl' \
  python experiments/011-heading-terms/terms_spacy.py
#+end_src

It reads and writes the books cache only.

* Not done

- The pairs rerun after the port: the "8 of 10" is the rule before it.
- A tagger on the JVM (OpenNLP, CoreNLP), which is what porting part of
  speech would mean.
- Headings that are captions. Manning's outlines list every "Listing 5.19"
  and "Figure 5.4", and "listing" is the second most shared term between
  two Manning books. They should be dropped before terms are made.
