#+title: 012 — concepts from each book's own index

* Source

=notes/books-shelf.org=: the books graph's terms are words from headings,
and the words beside "knowledge graph" were "completion", "interacting",
"building". A heading says what a chapter is called. An index says what an
author thought a reader would look up.

* Hypothesis

The index pages of a PDF, read with word positions, give a book's concepts
without a model: a main entry starts at its column's left edge and a
sub-entry is indented, so position alone separates "knowledge graph" from
"defined" beneath it.

* Gate

Decided before the run:

- an index is read for at least 20 of the 29 books;
- at least 300 concepts are listed by two books or more (the heading rule
  gives 191 shared phrases);
- of the workshop's subjects absent as heading terms (Gemini, BigQuery,
  Vertex AI), at least one appears.

* Result

Passed, 2026-10-09, with the entries' quality checked by eye and not
scored.

| Measure                              | Value |
|--------------------------------------+-------|
| Books with an Index heading          | 24 of 29 |
| Entries read                         | 9,940 |
| Concepts after joining names         | 8,288 |
| Concepts in two books or more        | 777   |
| Concepts in four books or more       | 132   |
| Gemini / BigQuery / Vertex AI        | 3 books / none / none |

No Python: the reader is =bb books index= (=bb/books.clj=), over
=pdftotext -bbox-layout=, and the join is =workshop.books/concept-name=.

* Log

1. Plain =pdftotext= loses indentation, and a sub-entry ("maintenance, 34")
   cannot be told from a main one. =-bbox-layout= keeps each line's x.
2. Left and right pages have different margins. Column edges are found
   for odd and even pages apart, over the whole index and not per page: a
   column that holds only sub-entries would otherwise take one as its edge.
3. First pattern for page numbers allowed roman numerals anywhere at the
   end of a line and ate the last letter of "index", "syntax" and
   "LangChain". A number now has to follow a comma or a space.
4. Eight Manning books carry the buyer's name and address on every page.
   Lines with it are dropped; the cache was checked for it after.
5. "RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)" in one index is
   "Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)" in another. The longer side of
   the parenthesis is kept, so both are one concept, in eight books.

* Not done

- Sub-entries. They are dropped, and with them "knowledge graph,
  construction of".
- "See" and "see also", which are the index's own edges between concepts.
- Scoring: nobody marked a sample of entries right or wrong. /Neo4j in
  Action/'s index has no page numbers and includes lines of code;
  /The Joy of Clojure/ leaks some sub-entries ("is data", "repetitive").
- The five books with no Index heading.
- Page numbers are the printed ones, not the PDF's, and are kept as text.
